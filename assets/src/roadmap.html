Retest #1 · D+7

Network rescan only. 3 fixed and verified (VPN CVE-2024-3400, SMBv1 on the HR file server, internet RDP on the jump host). 12 not retested stay open: web and manual findings need their own retest.

OT rule

Plant findings are never patched live. Contain in 72 h at the IT/OT boundary, then apply the permanent fix in the next approved window under Management of Change.

Personal data

When a P1/P2 hits an asset with personal data, the DPO gets a ticket to decide on GDPR Art. 33 notification (72 h) in parallel with the fix.
